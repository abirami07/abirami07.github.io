Data 100 · Summer 2026 · Projects A1 & A2

Cook County housing

I worked with property assessment data from Cook County, Illinois, first exploring the data and then building a model to predict housing sale prices.

What I worked on

  • Explored distributions and relationships in property records and considered the social context of the dataset.
  • Cleaned messy housing fields and engineered predictors, including features extracted from property descriptions.
  • Built and evaluated price prediction models, examining error metrics and model performance.

What I learned

Model accuracy is not the same as fairness. Comparing errors across housing price ranges showed me why an overall RMSE can hide uneven outcomes, and why the choice of error metric matters when a model could influence property assessments.
