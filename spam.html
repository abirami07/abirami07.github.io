Data 100 · Summer 2026 · Projects B1 & B2

Spam / ham classification

I worked on a text classification pipeline to distinguish spam emails from regular messages.

What I worked on

  • Explored differences between spam and ham using words and text-derived features.
  • Engineered numerical inputs from email text and trained logistic regression classifiers.
  • Used training/validation splits and evaluation metrics to examine model performance and refine features.

What I learned

Feature engineering made a noticeable difference in how well the model fit the training emails. I also learned that training accuracy alone does not tell you how a spam filter will perform on unseen messages, and that false positives and false negatives have different consequences.

Results

75.8%Initial classifier training accuracy
90.7%Final classifier training accuracy

These are training-set accuracies from Projects B1/B2, not validation or test accuracy. Improvement: approximately 15.0 percentage points.
